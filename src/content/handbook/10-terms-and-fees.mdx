## Company searches

**No hire, no fee on standard searches.** Most searches run on a success fee: a percentage of the hire's first-year salary. The exact percentage depends on how many roles we're filling and how we work together, so you get it in writing before anything starts.

Mission-critical searches and team builds can be set up differently, and we agree that in our first video meeting, not after.

I don't publish a fee percentage, because it genuinely depends on volume and the type of engagement. What I do promise is that the fee, the timeline and what I need from you are agreed in plain words before the search starts, and nothing changes halfway.

## What's included

Everything in [Included in every search](/handbook/07-included-in-every-search/): job descriptions, process advice, scheduling, candidate care, offer negotiation and onboarding support. No separate agreement, no extra invoice, no 50-CV dump.

## If the hire doesn't work out

If they leave or it clearly isn't working early on, I find a replacement at no extra fee. The window follows local market practice, usually the length of the probation period in the hire's country, and it's written into your agreement before we start.

## If we're not a fit

I'll tell you in our first video meeting and point you somewhere useful.

## Career Re:Work

EUR 179, one payment, no subscription. It is not a job guarantee. You can book a free 20-minute video meeting before paying. If I don't think I can help you, I'll tell you and you keep your money.

## Applying to my open roles

Free, always.
