## A recruiter in your corner for once

Normally I work for companies. With Career Re:Work I work for you. In one session we rebuild how you come across on paper, on LinkedIn, on camera and in interviews. Then I take your profile to the market myself and recommend you to the people who make hiring decisions, including at companies that don't have a job ad out.

**EUR 179, one payment, no subscription.** If you're unsure, book a free 20-minute video meeting first and ask me anything.

## What you get

### The concierge part: I pitch you to the people who decide

This is the bit no job board can do. I go through the market, find companies where your profile makes sense, and recommend you directly to the CTO, Head of Engineering, founder or product lead. Not to a careers inbox.

- Companies hiring right now, and ones that haven't opened a role yet.
- Anywhere in the world, not only where I'm currently recruiting.
- You arrive as a specific recommendation with context, not a cold CV.

### 1. Discovery session (45 minutes, 1:1)

Where you are, what's not working, where to aim. An honest read on your market fit, which roles to chase and which to drop.

### 2. CV rewrite

Your real experience, made clear and relevant. Your story, not a template. Outcomes instead of duties.

### 3. LinkedIn update

A profile that sounds like you and gets found. Headline built for recruiter search, experience rewritten around results.

### 4. Video answers

2-3 short recordings that show how you think. Prompts for your target roles, and they're yours to reuse anywhere.

### 5. Interview prep

A mock interview with direct feedback. Stories that sound like you. Sharp, not scripted.

### And it doesn't expire

You get a private Slack channel with me for your whole job search, not for a week. Applying somewhere on your own? Got an interview tomorrow or an offer on the table? Message me and I'll help, until you've landed.

## The truth rule

Everything on your CV has to be true and defendable in an interview. I make your real experience clear, not bigger. Most CVs repeat the job ad the person was hired from:

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Responsible for backend development and maintenance of microservices in an agile team. | Moved the payments service to event-driven processing in Go and owned on-call for it, so checkout incidents stopped waking up the whole team. |

This is an illustrative example, not a real client CV.

## How it runs

1. **You book.** Pay once, pick a time for the session.
2. **We talk for 45 minutes.** We go through your experience, what's been failing, and agree where you should aim.
3. **We rebuild your materials.** CV, LinkedIn, video answers and interview stories, all shaped for the roles we picked together.
4. **I go to the market for you.** I use AI tools to map companies where you'd fit, then I pick the ones worth it and recommend you to the decision maker myself.
5. **Things start moving.** Most people see real conversations within 2 to 4 weeks. How fast depends on your market, seniority and how much of the work you put in.
6. **I stay with you until you land.** Your private Slack channel stays open for the whole search, including the applications you send on your own, the interviews and the offers.

## Is this for you?

**Yes, if:**

- You work in tech, product or design and your CV keeps disappearing into the void.
- You can take direct feedback without getting defensive.
- You're ready to do the work, not pay and hope.
- You'd rather have five good conversations than 200 applications.

**No, if:**

- You want someone to mass-apply to 100 jobs for you.
- You won't record a few short video answers.
- You expect a job guarantee. There isn't one.
- You want a CV template rather than a real rewrite.
