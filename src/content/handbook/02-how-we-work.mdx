## Principles

- Work asynchronously by default.
- Write decisions down.
- Be direct, specific, and useful.
- Protect candidate privacy and client confidentiality.
- Prioritise quality of evidence over speed of activity.

## Communication

We use clear written updates, shared documents, and structured meeting notes. A meeting should produce a decision, a next action, an owner, and a deadline.

## Response expectations

| Situation | Expected response |
|---|---|
| Client request during an active search | Within one business day |
| Candidate question | Within one business day |
| Urgent interview or offer issue | Same business day |
| Regular search update | Weekly, unless agreed otherwise |

## Decision-making

Before making a decision, identify:

1. The goal.
2. The available evidence.
3. The trade-offs.
4. The owner.
5. The review date.

If a decision affects a client, candidate, budget, or public-facing material, document it.
