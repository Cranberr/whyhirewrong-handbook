## Core services

- Technology candidate sourcing
- Market and talent mapping
- Candidate screening and assessment
- Shortlist preparation
- Interview process design
- Recruitment operations support
- Employer outreach and talent-brand support

## What clients receive

Every engagement should produce clear, usable outputs:

- A calibrated role profile
- A market map or sourcing strategy
- A structured candidate scorecard
- A documented shortlist
- Candidate feedback and market intelligence
- Clear next actions and owners

## Engagement boundaries

We are clear about what is included before work begins: target roles, locations, seniority, sourcing channels, deliverables, communication cadence, and decision rights.

If scope changes, we update the agreement rather than silently changing the work.
