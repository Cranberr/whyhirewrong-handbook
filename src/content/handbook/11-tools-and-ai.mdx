You should know what touches your hiring and your candidates' data. Everything runs through my ATS, so it's one connected workflow, not ten tabs and a spreadsheet.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| Teamtailor + Co-pilot | My ATS and the centre of everything. Co-pilot, its built-in AI, screens and summarises applications. Every candidate, note, video answer and message lives here. |
| Candidate presentation | Clients review people through a Teamtailor link: CV, video answers and my notes together, in the browser. No CVs flying around in email attachments. |
| Juicebox | Finding and reaching the right people directly, so a search isn't limited to whoever happens to see a job ad. |
| Perplexity | Market research for hiring companies: hiring trends, competitors, salary benchmarks and talent markets. I also use it to analyse GitHub profiles and portfolios and flag potential inconsistencies or fraud signals for my review. |
| Littlebird + MCP | My AI assistant, connected to my tools through MCP, so context from video meetings, email and the ATS stays in sync and nothing falls through. |

## What AI never does

It never decides who reaches you, never writes your rejections for me, and never replaces the conversation. Every person on your shortlist talked to me first.

**Do you use AI to pick candidates?** No. Teamtailor Co-pilot removes noise and summarises applications, and other tools speed up research. Who goes on your shortlist is my call, made after talking to them.
