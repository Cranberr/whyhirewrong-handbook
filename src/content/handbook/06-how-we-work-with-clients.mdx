## Intake

Every search starts with a role-calibration conversation.

We clarify the business problem, team context, required outcomes, must-have requirements, target companies, hiring timeline, process, decision-makers, and budget.

## Search cadence

- **Week 1:** Role calibration, scorecard, sourcing strategy, and market map.
- **Week 2 onward:** Outreach, screening, and weekly progress updates.
- **Shortlist stage:** Structured candidate recommendations with evidence.
- **Interview stage:** Feedback collection and process support.
- **Offer stage:** Candidate-motivation and risk assessment.

## Weekly update

A useful update includes:

- Candidates identified.
- Candidates contacted.
- Responses received.
- Candidates screened.
- Candidates progressed or declined.
- Market feedback.
- Risks and recommended actions.

## Our commitments

- We give honest market feedback, even when it is uncomfortable.
- We flag process issues early.
- We protect confidential client information.
- We do not submit candidates without a documented rationale.
