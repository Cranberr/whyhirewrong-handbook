## Principles

- Source for evidence of capability, not keyword matches.
- Understand the market before contacting candidates.
- Personalise outreach based on relevant experience and motivation.
- Track what works and improve the approach.

## Sourcing process

1. Define the role scorecard.
2. Identify target companies, skills, and adjacent talent pools.
3. Build a sourcing strategy and Boolean searches.
4. Identify candidates and record relevant evidence.
5. Prioritise candidates for outreach.
6. Send tailored messages.
7. Track response rates and refine messaging.

## Outreach standards

A good outreach message is short, specific, and respectful of the candidate’s time.

It should explain:

- Why we contacted them.
- What makes the opportunity relevant.
- What the next step would be.
- That they can ask questions or decline without pressure.

## What we avoid

- Generic copy-paste messages.
- Exaggerated role descriptions.
- Contacting people without a relevant reason.
- Treating a lack of response as consent to repeated contact.
