## For companies: I find you people who already did the work

- Search live on day one, first qualified candidates within 4-5 working days, hires closed in 3-6 weeks.
- A shortlist of 3 to 5 people I'd hire myself, each with video answers and my honest notes.
- Engineering, AI, product, design and tech leadership roles, anywhere in the world.
- On a standard search you pay only if you hire someone from the list.

The full method is in [The company search protocol](/handbook/04-company-search-protocol/).

## For job seekers: Career Re:Work

I stop screening you and start representing you.

- One session where we fix your CV, LinkedIn, video answers and interview stories.
- Then I pitch you myself to decision makers at companies that could use you, hiring or not.
- EUR 179, one payment. Separate from the roles I'm hiring for.

Details in [Career Re:Work](/handbook/09-career-rework/).

## For job seekers: my open roles

Real briefs, real companies, honest salary bands, pulled live from my ATS. Applying is free and you'll hear back either way. Open roles are listed at [whyhirewrong.com](https://whyhirewrong.com/) and on my [Teamtailor careers page](https://whyhirewrong.teamtailor.com/jobs).

Applying to my open roles and buying Career Re:Work are two separate things. One is free and tied to a specific client brief. The other is me working on your behalf across the wider market.
