## Our promise

Candidates should always know where they stand.

We provide clear information, respect their time, and communicate decisions promptly.

## What we do

- Explain the role and process honestly.
- Ask before sharing a candidate’s profile with a client.
- Give candidates an opportunity to ask questions.
- Provide an update after each meaningful stage.
- Give useful feedback where possible.
- Keep candidate information confidential.

## What we avoid

- Sending candidates into unclear processes.
- Making promises we cannot control.
- Sharing personal data without consent.
- Disappearing after an interview or rejection.
- Treating candidates as inventory.

## Feedback

When we decline a candidate, we aim to explain the decision in a respectful and specific way. We do not provide misleading feedback or blame the candidate for a poor process.
