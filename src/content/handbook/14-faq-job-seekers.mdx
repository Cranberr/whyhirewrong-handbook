### Is Career Re:Work the same as applying to your open roles?

No. Applying to the roles I'm hiring for is free and completely separate. Career Re:Work is me working on your behalf across the wider market, including companies that have no job ad out.

### Which companies will you pitch me to?

The ones that make sense for your profile and the roles we agree on, anywhere in the world. Some are already hiring, some haven't opened a role yet. I go to the person who decides, not to a careers inbox.

### Will you make my CV look better than I am?

No. Everything on it has to be true and something you can defend in an interview. I've seen what happens to inflated CVs in interviews. I make your real experience clear, not bigger.

### Is the ATS rejecting my CV?

Almost certainly not. An ATS is a database. It doesn't read your CV and score you. The only automatic rejections come from screening questions like work permit or salary. Usually you're not rejected, you're invisible because your CV doesn't use the words people search for. That's fixable.

### How long does my support last?

It doesn't expire. You have my support for your whole job search, even if you find roles and apply on your own. Interview tomorrow, offer to think through, a recruiter you're not sure about: message me.

### How do I reach you?

Directly, on a private Slack channel just for you. No ticket system, no assistant in between.

### Is it a job guarantee?

No. Nobody honest can promise you a job. What I can promise is that you'll show up sharper, and that people with hiring power will hear about you from me.

### How fast will something happen?

Most people see real conversations within 2 to 4 weeks. It depends on your market, your seniority and how much of the work you put in.

### Do I have to record videos?

Yes, 2 or 3 short ones, a few minutes each. They feel strange at first. Then they often become the thing that gets you the interview, and you keep them for every future application.

### I'm not in Poland. Does that matter?

No. I work with people and companies worldwide, all remotely.

### Can I ask questions before paying?

Yes. Book the free 20-minute video meeting. If I don't think I can help you, I'll tell you and you keep your money.

### Will I hear back if I apply to one of your roles?

Yes. Applying is free and you'll hear back either way. If it's a no, you hear from me within 5 business days, with a reason.
