## Engagement principles

We prefer clear scope, defined deliverables, and honest expectations over vague retainers.

Before an engagement begins, both sides should understand:

- The role and business problem.
- The target market and hiring timeline.
- The deliverables.
- The communication cadence.
- The decision-maker.
- The budget and payment terms.
- What happens if the role, process, or scope changes.

## Engagement models

### Market mapping

Best for companies that need evidence before committing to a search.

Typical deliverables:

- Target-company map.
- Talent-pool overview.
- Compensation and market-context observations.
- Recommended sourcing strategy.
- Shortlist of high-potential people to approach.

### Sourcing sprint

Best for a defined role where the client has a clear process and interview capacity.

Typical deliverables:

- Role scorecard.
- Sourcing strategy.
- Candidate pipeline.
- Structured screening notes.
- Shortlist with recommendations.

### End-to-end recruitment support

Best for companies that need support from role calibration through offer stage.

Typical deliverables:

- Role calibration and scorecard.
- Market map and sourcing plan.
- Candidate outreach and screening.
- Interview-process support.
- Candidate feedback and offer-stage guidance.

## What is included

A proposal should state exactly what is included. For example:

- Number of roles.
- Sourcing channels.
- Screening interviews.
- Shortlist size or delivery milestone.
- Weekly updates.
- Interview scheduling support, if applicable.
- Reporting format.

## What is not included

Unless explicitly agreed, we do not include:

- Job-board advertising spend.
- Paid LinkedIn campaigns.
- Background checks.
- Legal or immigration advice.
- Guarantee of a hire.
- Unlimited role changes or restarts.

## Scope changes

If the role changes materially, the process changes, or the client adds requirements after the search has started, we pause and recalibrate.

A material change can affect timeline, strategy, candidate pool, and price. We document the revised scope before continuing.
