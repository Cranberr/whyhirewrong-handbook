## Fast because it all runs at once, not one channel after another

You're needed for two of the four steps. The first qualified candidates land in your inbox within 4-5 working days, and the rest keep coming while you interview.

| When | Step | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Brief video meeting | You and me |
| Day 1 | Every channel goes live | Machines and me |
| Days 1-5 | Real conversations | Me |
| From day 4-5 | First candidates, one link | You |
| Weeks 3-6 | Offer signed | You, the candidate and me |

## 1. Brief video meeting (day 0, you and me)

Thirty minutes on what the role really needs. If the salary, title or wish list won't work in this market, I'll say so here, not three weeks in.

## 2. Every channel at once (day 1, machines and me)

Job ad, direct search, my network, referrals and outreach all go live the same day. Bots and fake profiles get filtered, and everyone left gets a proof task and video questions. See [Screening and verification](/handbook/05-screening-and-verification/).

## 3. Real conversations (days 1-5, me)

I talk to every person who passes, check motivation, salary and notice period, then keep them warm and informed until you decide.

## 4. First candidates, one link (from day 4-5, you)

Within 4-5 working days you get a Teamtailor link: CV, video answers and my notes for each person, in your browser. It builds to a shortlist of 3 to 5, and I stay on through offer and onboarding. See [The shortlist](/handbook/06-the-shortlist/).

## What I hire and where

Worldwide, remote, hybrid or on-site. Engineering, AI and ML, low-latency C++, DevOps and cloud, QA, product, design and tech leadership. Recent searches covered Poland, Germany, Spain, the UK, France, Estonia, Brazil, the US and Canada.

## Timelines

- The search starts the day we agree.
- First qualified candidates within 4-5 working days.
- The full cycle, from brief to signed offer, usually closes in 3 to 6 weeks.
- If the market can't produce what the brief asks for, I tell you in the first week.
