## Before a client engagement

- Confirm the role, requirements, process, and timeline.
- Identify the decision-maker.
- Agree deliverables and communication cadence.
- Check for conflicts, compliance needs, and data-protection requirements.

## Before a shortlist

- Confirm every candidate has been screened against the scorecard.
- Record evidence for each must-have requirement.
- Confirm motivation, availability, notice period, and compensation expectations.
- Identify risks and open questions.
- Explain the recommendation in plain language.

## Before sending outreach

- Confirm the message is relevant to the person.
- Remove unsupported claims.
- Check spelling, role title, company name, and links.
- Ensure there is a clear, low-pressure next step.

## Writing standard

Write like a competent practitioner, not a corporate brochure.

- Use short sentences.
- Remove filler words.
- Be specific.
- Show evidence.
- Avoid vague claims such as “world-class”, “passionate”, or “high-quality” unless they are defined.
