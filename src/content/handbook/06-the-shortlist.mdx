## One link, everything in one place

Clients review people through a Teamtailor link opened in the browser. Each person comes with their CV, video answers and my notes together, so the team can review and comment without email chains or attachments.

A shortlist is 3 to 5 people I'd hire myself. You read 3 to 5 profiles instead of 300.

## What every profile contains

1. **Verification status:** proof task done, video answers recorded, GitHub or portfolio checked, references and notice period confirmed.
2. **Video answers** recorded for this role.
3. **Detailed verification** from my video meeting: what they built, what they owned, what the outcome was.
4. **Strengths** against the requirements.
5. **Watch-outs.** The gaps, said out loud.
6. **What they're looking for:** motivation, salary expectations and availability.
7. **My recommendation:** the reasons to meet them, the reasons to pause, and the specific areas your team should explore next.

## Example

This example is adapted for a public preview.

**Senior Frontend Engineer, 8 years, Poland, remote or hybrid**

- **Verification:** 8 years of Angular and TypeScript. Main author of an enterprise codebase. Built the AI-assisted workflow himself: agent instructions, mandatory diff review and quality gates after finding agent-written tests that asserted nothing. Moved releases from 1-3 months to a predictable 2-week cadence.
- **Strengths:** Angular 5 to 21, signals, complex UI, mentoring, and a real AI workflow with Copilot and Claude review.
- **Watch-outs:** Cypress experience is dated, no production Node.js, live coding needs practice. Best used where Angular depth and AI-assisted delivery matter more than DevOps breadth.
- **Looking for:** senior frontend or AI-product engineering. Remote or hybrid, B2B. Wants a product-led team, not a ticket queue.
- **My recommendation:** strong frontend engineer with a real AI workflow, not just AI keywords. He can tell when generated code is wrong, which is the part most teams struggle to hire for. Shortlist for Angular-heavy product teams, paired with a strong QA or DevOps setup.

## Confidentiality

Profiles shown publicly are anonymised. Full context goes to a client only with the candidate's say-so.
