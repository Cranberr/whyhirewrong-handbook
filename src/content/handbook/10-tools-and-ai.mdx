## Principle

AI helps us research, organise, draft, and analyse. It does not make final hiring decisions.

Every candidate recommendation, rejection, and client-facing conclusion remains a human decision based on evidence.

## Where AI helps

- Researching companies, technologies, and market context.
- Structuring role requirements and interview questions.
- Drafting outreach messages for human review.
- Summarising notes into scorecards.
- Identifying patterns in sourcing performance.
- Preparing client updates and reports.

## Where humans decide

- Whether a candidate meets a requirement.
- Whether evidence is sufficient.
- Whether to progress, reject, or hold a candidate.
- What is communicated to a client or candidate.
- Whether a search strategy needs to change.

## Data rules

- Do not enter confidential client data, candidate personal data, or credentials into tools without checking permissions and data-protection requirements.
- Do not upload CVs or candidate profiles to an AI tool unless the tool and process are approved for that purpose.
- Remove identifying information where full data is unnecessary.
- Keep a record of the tool, purpose, and approval for sensitive use cases.

## Quality control

Before using AI-assisted output:

1. Check facts against the source.
2. Remove generic or unsupported claims.
3. Ensure the message is relevant to the recipient.
4. Confirm that no confidential data has been exposed.
5. Make the final decision as a human.
