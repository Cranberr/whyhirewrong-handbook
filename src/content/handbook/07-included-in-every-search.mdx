The shortlist is the visible part. Around it I do the work that usually falls on your team, from the job ad to the first weeks after signing, so you can spend your time interviewing. It's part of every search, not an add-on.

## 1. Candidates who stay in the race

I prepare people for each stage, keep them motivated and tell them where things stand. Good candidates don't drift off to another offer because nobody followed up for two weeks.

## 2. Scheduling and the loose ends

If you want, I book the interviews, chase the slots and keep track of notice periods, counteroffers and everything else that matters. You turn up and interview.

## 3. Job descriptions that work

Inclusive, well-engineered ads written for the market as it is, with honest salary bands and the proof task built in. The right people apply, and the wrong ones filter themselves out.

## 4. A better hiring process

Too many rounds, unclear owners, a take-home nobody reviews. If I see something slowing you down or losing you people, I'll tell you and help you fix it.

## 5. Feedback culture

I help your team give candidates fast, specific feedback. It keeps your employer brand clean and it's why I can say nobody I've worked with got ghosted.

## 6. Plain terms, no jargon

No corporate rules, no small print to decode. Before we start you know the fee, the timeline and what I need from you, all in plain words.

## 7. Offer stage, both sides

I negotiate with you and with the candidate and bring up what nobody says out loud: the counteroffer coming, the notice period, the thing they actually care about. Fewer offers fall apart at the last step.

## 8. Onboarding, prepared

Signing isn't the finish line. I help prepare the start, stay in touch with your new hire through notice and the first weeks, and flag anything that could go wrong before it does.

## 9. A private Slack channel

You and I talk on our own Slack channel, not in an email thread. Candidates get theirs too. I'm always there to answer questions, and nothing waits for a weekly status meeting.

**All of this is included in every search. No separate agreement, no extra invoice.**
