Pulled straight from my ATS across engineering, AI, C++, leadership, design and sales searches in 2026. About 1 in 8 applications gets past my first screen, and the reasons are surprisingly consistent.

| Function | Applications | Past my first screen | What I learned |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engineering and AI | 1,000+ | 7% | Production agent and eval experience is the rare signal. One bulk-applied full-stack role pulled 218 applications and nobody made it past screening. |
| Low-latency C++ | 100+ | 9% | Real HFT and exchange-connectivity people exist, mostly in the UK and Benelux, mostly contractors. Generic C++ CVs vastly outnumber them. |
| Tech leadership | 514 | 10% | Directors with cloud, AI adoption and M&A or compliance behind them progressed. VP-level ads drew the fewest serious applicants. |
| Product design | 385 | 16% | Almost every strong designer now lists design systems and AI tools. Designers who have actually shipped an AI interface are rare. |
| Sales and BD | 446 | 21% | All three hires had a niche (life sciences, AI and video, defence) and two-plus languages. Generic sales CVs almost never moved. |
