## Principle

We assess evidence, not impressions.

A candidate is not “strong” because they sound confident. They are strong when their experience, capability, motivation, and constraints align with the role requirements.

## Role scorecard

Before screening begins, define:

- Must-have requirements.
- Important but trainable requirements.
- Deal-breakers.
- Success measures for the first 6 to 12 months.
- Interview questions mapped to each requirement.

## Screening process

1. Review the candidate’s experience against the scorecard.
2. Hold a structured screening conversation.
3. Record evidence for each requirement.
4. Identify motivation, availability, notice period, and salary expectations.
5. Decide whether to progress, hold, or decline.
6. Share a clear recommendation with the client.

## Shortlist standard

A candidate enters a shortlist only when we can explain:

- Why they fit the role.
- Which requirements they meet, with evidence.
- Any risks or gaps.
- Their motivation and availability.
- Their compensation expectations and notice period.
- The recommended next step.

## Candidate experience

Candidates receive a clear explanation of the process, realistic timelines, and an update after each meaningful stage. We do not ghost candidates.
