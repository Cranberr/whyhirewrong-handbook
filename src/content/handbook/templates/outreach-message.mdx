## Principles

- Lead with relevance.
- Be specific.
- Do not oversell.
- Make declining easy.

<Template title="Outreach message">

Hi [Name],

I came across your experience at [Company] and was interested in your work with [specific technology, product, or achievement].

I am working with [company type or client context] on a [role title] opportunity focused on [key challenge or technology]. It may be relevant because of your experience with [specific relevant area].

The role is [location/remote setup] and involves [one or two meaningful details].

Would you be open to a short conversation? If this is not relevant, no problem at all; just let me know and I will not follow up.

Best,  
[Your name]

</Template>

## Do not send

- Generic messages with no role-specific reason.
- Claims about compensation or flexibility that have not been confirmed.
- Multiple follow-ups after a clear decline.
- Messages that pressure a candidate to respond.
