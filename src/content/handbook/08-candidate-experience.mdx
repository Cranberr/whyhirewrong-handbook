## Nobody gets left on read

Every applicant hears where they stand. Candidates I reject hear from me within 5 business days, with a reason. A client's employer brand travels with every person who applies, and I treat it that way.

## During a process

- I prepare people for each stage and tell them what comes next.
- I check motivation, salary and notice period in our video meeting.
- Candidates get their own private Slack channel with me.
- A busy week on the client's side doesn't leave candidates guessing.

## Applying to my open roles

Applying is free and you'll hear back either way. Every role is a real brief from a real company, with an honest salary band.

## Proof tasks and video answers

Proof tasks are short, written for the role and announced in the job ad. Video answers take a few minutes each. They exist because one application written by AI tells a company almost nothing, and they give real candidates a way to stand out from the noise.

## Privacy and consent

Candidate profiles shown in public are anonymised. Full context is shared with a client only with the candidate's say-so. Candidate data lives in my ATS, not in email attachments.
