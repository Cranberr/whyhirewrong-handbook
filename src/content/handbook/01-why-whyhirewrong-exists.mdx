## Hire proof, not promises

I'm Hubert Warszta and I've been hiring engineers, product people and designers since 2012. These days a big part of every inbox is written by bots, so I let software deal with the noise and spend my own time on the bit it's bad at: talking to real people and telling you honestly who is worth hiring.

WhyHireWrong? is a one-person recruiting practice based in Warsaw and working worldwide. When this handbook says "I", it means me. There is no team behind the curtain.

## You don't have a talent shortage. You have a noise problem.

Applying was always free. Now AI does it for people in seconds, so one application tells you almost nothing. The good people are still out there. In an ATS they just look exactly like everyone else, which is why I filter on effort and proof before I look at CVs.

What my inbox looked like in autumn 2026:

- **158** applications from auto-apply bots, flagged in a single sweep across my open roles (September 2026).
- **129 of 134** LinkedIn messages from job seekers were the same AI template with the keywords swapped (October 2026).
- **2 of 118** applicants for a Staff Backend role recorded the short video answers the ad asked for (2026).

That Staff Backend role pulled in 118 applications from the US. Some had phone numbers typed into the salary field. On another search two "senior engineers" applied with one phone number under two different names.

If that pile lands on your desk you lose a week and decide nobody good is looking, which isn't true. So I start with proof, and the CV is the last thing I look at, not the first.

## Why the name

I was a wrong hire once. That's where WhyHireWrong? comes from.

The name came to me in about a second, no brainstorming, because I'd been on the wrong end of it myself. Since then I've watched wrong hires happen from both sides, companies picking the wrong person and people picking the wrong job, and most of the time one honest conversation early on would have prevented it.

That conversation is most of what I sell. The tools around it are there so I have time to have it with every person who deserves one.
