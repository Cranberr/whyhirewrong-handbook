## The problem

Too many hiring decisions are made from keywords, vague job descriptions, unstructured interviews, and pressure to fill a role quickly.

The result is predictable: long processes, weak shortlists, poor candidate experience, and expensive mis-hires.

## What we believe

- Hiring is a research and decision-making process, not a volume game.
- A good shortlist explains *why* each candidate fits, not merely that they exist.
- Candidates deserve clarity, respect, and timely feedback.
- Clients deserve evidence, market context, and honest advice.
- Recruitment should reduce hiring risk, not create more administrative work.

## What WhyHireWrong? does

WhyHireWrong? helps technology companies make better hiring decisions through targeted sourcing, structured candidate assessment, market research, and practical recruitment support.

We work best with teams that care about quality of hire, candidate experience, and making defensible hiring decisions.

## What we do not do

- Send unvetted CVs to create the appearance of activity.
- Treat candidate data as a commodity.
- Recommend a candidate solely because they are available.
- Hide market reality to win an assignment.
- Leave clients or candidates without updates.
