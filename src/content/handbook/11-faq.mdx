## For clients

### How quickly can you start?

We can usually begin with a role-calibration session shortly after the engagement is confirmed. The first deliverables are normally a calibrated scorecard, sourcing strategy, and market map.

### Do you guarantee a hire?

No responsible recruiter can guarantee that a specific candidate will accept an offer. We commit to a defined process, transparent communication, and evidence-based shortlists.

### What do you need from us?

Clear role context, interview capacity, timely feedback, access to the decision-maker, and agreement on the process and timeline.

### How do you handle candidate data?

We collect and process candidate information only for a legitimate recruitment purpose, with appropriate confidentiality and data-protection safeguards.

## For candidates

### Will you share my CV without asking?

No. We ask before sharing a candidate’s profile with a client.

### Will I receive feedback?

We aim to update candidates after each meaningful stage. Where possible, we provide specific and respectful feedback.

### Can I ask questions about a role?

Yes. Candidates should understand the role, process, compensation range where available, and next steps before deciding whether to proceed.

### What if I am not interested?

Tell us. We will stop the process and do not treat a lack of response as consent to continued contact.
