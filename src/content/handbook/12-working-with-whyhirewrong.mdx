## Client enquiries

Send a short brief including:

- Company and team context.
- Role title and location or remote setup.
- Key technologies and seniority.
- Hiring timeline.
- Process and interview capacity.
- Budget or compensation range, if available.
- Main hiring challenge.

We respond with a practical recommendation: whether a market map, sourcing sprint, or broader recruitment support is the best starting point.

## Candidate introductions

Candidates can contact us with:

- A CV or LinkedIn profile.
- Preferred role type and seniority.
- Location or remote preference.
- Notice period.
- Compensation expectations, if comfortable sharing.
- Types of companies or technologies they want to avoid.

## Partnerships

We are open to partnerships with technology companies, hiring platforms, career communities, and specialist service providers where the collaboration creates genuine value for clients or candidates.
