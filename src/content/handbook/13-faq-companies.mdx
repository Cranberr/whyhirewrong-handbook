### How are you different from a normal agency?

Most agencies forward CVs. I only send people who already did work for your role, recorded video answers and passed a real conversation with me. You get 3 to 5 profiles with my honest notes, not a pile to sort through.

### Do you help with more than finding candidates?

Yes, and it's included. I prepare candidates and keep them motivated and informed, schedule interviews if you want me to, help write inclusive job descriptions that reflect the market, and help improve your hiring process and feedback culture where it needs it.

### Do you help at the offer stage?

Yes. I negotiate with both sides and bring up what neither side sees: counteroffers, notice periods, what the candidate really cares about. Then I help prepare onboarding and stay in touch through the first weeks.

### How do we stay in touch during a search?

On a private Slack channel, just you and me. Candidates get their own private channel with me too. I'm always available there for questions, so nobody waits days for an email.

### Is that extra help charged separately?

No. Job descriptions, process advice, scheduling, candidate care, offer negotiation and onboarding support are a normal part of every search. There's no separate agreement and no extra invoice.

### How do you charge?

Most searches run on a success fee: a percentage of the hire's first-year salary, and no hire, no fee. The exact percentage depends on how many roles we're filling and how we work together, so you get it in writing before anything starts. Mission-critical searches and team builds can be set up differently, and we agree that in our first video meeting, not after.

### What if the hire doesn't work out?

If they leave or it clearly isn't working early on, I find a replacement at no extra fee. The window follows local market practice, usually the length of the probation period in the hire's country, and it's written into your agreement before we start.

### Who keeps candidates engaged while we're busy?

Me. I keep every candidate informed about where things stand and what comes next, so a busy week on your side doesn't cost you your best person.

### What does working together look like on paper?

Short and in plain language. No corporate rules or jargon. Before we start you know the fee, the timeline and what I need from you, and nothing changes halfway.

### How fast will we see people?

The search starts the day we agree, with all my sourcing channels running at once. You see the first qualified candidates within 4-5 working days. The full cycle, from brief to signed offer, usually closes in 3 to 6 weeks. If the market can't produce what the brief asks for, I'll tell you in the first week.

### Which tools do you use, and for what?

Teamtailor with its built-in Co-pilot for screening and the whole candidate pipeline, Juicebox for direct sourcing and outreach, Perplexity for market research, and Littlebird connected through MCP to keep everything in sync. It's all integrated with the ATS, and I'm happy to walk you through it.

### How do you present candidates?

Through a Teamtailor link you open in your browser. Each person comes with their CV, video answers and my notes in one place, so your team can review and comment without email chains or attachments.

### How do you catch bots and fake applicants?

Software flags the obvious stuff: auto-apply tools, throwaway email domains, copy-paste answers, broken LinkedIn links, shared phone numbers. Then the proof task and the video answers filter out anyone who isn't real or isn't serious. Nobody reaches you without a live conversation with me.

### Do you check what's on the CV?

Yes, in the video meeting. I ask who owned the decision, what broke and what it cost. Inflated claims usually fall apart in about ninety seconds, and I write down what I found in my notes for you.

### Won't a proof task scare good people away?

It scares away people who weren't going to take your role seriously. The tasks are short and written for your role. People who actually want the job do them, and the shortlist gets better, not shorter.

### Do you use AI to pick candidates?

No. Teamtailor Co-pilot, built into my ATS, removes noise and summarises applications, and other tools speed up research. Who goes on your shortlist is my call, made after talking to them, and I put my name on it.

### Where and what do you hire?

Worldwide, remote, hybrid or on-site. Engineering, AI and ML, low-latency C++, DevOps and cloud, QA, product, design and tech leadership. Recent searches covered Poland, Germany, Spain, the UK, France, Estonia, Brazil, the US and Canada.

### What happens to candidates you reject?

They hear from me within 5 business days, with a reason. Your employer brand travels with every person who applies, and I treat it that way.

### What if we're not a fit?

I'll tell you in our first video meeting and point you somewhere useful. Saying no fast saves us both money.
