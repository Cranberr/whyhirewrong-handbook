## Proof first, CV last

A CV tells you what someone claims. I bring you the work, the person and the context behind it, so your first interview starts further ahead. Every search follows the same order: role brief, work samples, video context, human assessment.

## Software is fast at saying no. A person should be the one saying yes.

Screening runs on Teamtailor Co-pilot, the AI built into my ATS, and every other tool plugs into the same system, so nothing gets copied between apps or lost. That buys me hours, and I spend them on video meetings instead of spreadsheets.

### What the machines do: the sorting

1. Teamtailor Co-pilot screens every application inside my ATS. Bots, throwaway email domains and copy-paste answers get flagged before I read a single CV.
2. Check proof tasks: GitHub timestamp added, videos recorded, questions actually answered.
3. Map companies that are hiring, raising or growing, so I know where people could land.
4. Keep ATS, email and notes in sync so nobody falls through a crack.

One integrated workflow. Fast, tireless, and never the one who decides.

### What I do myself: the conversation

- Push back on the brief when salary, title or wish list don't match the market.
- Watch every video and talk to every finalist before a company sees them.
- Test every CV claim in a video meeting: who owned it, what broke, what it cost.
- Tell every applicant where they stand. Nobody gets left on read.

Slow on purpose. This part is the job.

## Honesty early, not three weeks in

- If the salary, title or wish list won't work in this market, I say so in the brief video meeting.
- If the market can't produce what the brief asks for, I tell you in the first week.
- If I'm not the right person for what you need, I say so in the first video meeting and point you to someone who is. Saying no fast saves us both money.

## Human judgement stays in the loop

My recommendation includes the reasons to meet someone and the reasons to pause. It's evidence to support your decision, not an automated verdict, and I put my name on it.
